Mahj Night Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Mahj Night is a scorekeeper for mahjong game nights. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: it stays on your device, and we never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Mahj Night has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send anything you enter to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved only on your iPhone or iPad:

  • the names, colors and emoji you give your players, and your groups and house rules;
  • your game nights, hands, results and settle-up history;
  • any photos you add to a game night;
  • a note of how many free game nights have been used, kept so the free allowance can't be reused.

If you use iCloud or computer backups for your device, this information may be included in those backups, which are run by Apple under your Apple Account and its own privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you tap "Take a photo". If you choose photos from your library, the app receives only the pictures you pick. Photos you add are stored on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it. When you tap a Share button (to share a night's result, a photo, or a copy of your records), your device's own share sheet opens and you decide where it goes.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock everything" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase has been made.

Money

Mahj Night only keeps score. No money moves through the app.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

Deleting a player, a game night, a group or a photo in the app removes it from your device. Deleting the app removes everything it has saved, except the small note of free game nights used.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
