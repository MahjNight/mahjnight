Mahj Nights

The scorekeeper for your mahjong game night.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone and iPad. Free to try.

Tap who won. We do the sums.

  • Log a hand in three taps: who won, who threw it, what it was worth.
  • Everyone's running total, at their seat around the table.
  • At the end of the night, the fewest payments that square everybody up.
  • Made a mistake? Undo it in one tap.
  • Big buttons and large type, easy to read across the table.

Made for your group

Your house rules, your stakes (or points only), tables of two to five, guests welcome, and stats and a leaderboard that build up night after night. Share the night's result with the group as a picture.

Private, and free to try

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone. Try it free for a week or three game nights. One small purchase unlocks it for good, with no subscription.
